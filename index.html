01 / Brand introduction

Four circles.
One long road.

Audi’s identity is rooted in German automotive engineering, an ambitious engineer and a union of four historic marques.

GERMANY · AUTOMOTIVE HISTORY

From Horch to Audi

August Horch founded A. Horch & Cie. in Cologne on 14 November 1899. After leaving that company, he established a new car company in 1909 in Zwickau, Saxony. Unable to use his surname again, he adopted its Latin meaning: audi—“listen.”

On 29 June 1932, Audi, DKW, Horch and the automotive division of Wanderer merged to form Auto Union. The four interlocking rings represent those four marques. The modern Audi brand grew from that history; the rings do not represent four current car divisions.

After the Second World War, Auto Union was re-established in Ingolstadt. The Audi name returned to a production car in 1965. Today, AUDI AG is headquartered in Germany and sells vehicles internationally, with model ranges and specifications adapted to each market.

BATTERY & MOTORS

High-voltage drive

Battery-electric Audis use lithium-ion high-voltage batteries and electric motors. Battery capacity, motor count, output and driven wheels vary by configuration.

CHARGING

Conditions matter

Peak DC power and 10–80% times are quoted under particular conditions. Charger capability, battery temperature and state of charge affect actual results.

ENVIRONMENT

Look at the lifecycle

Battery EVs have no tailpipe CO₂ while driving. Total lifecycle emissions also depend on vehicle and battery production and the electricity mix used for charging.

EVERYDAY DRIVING

Regenerative braking

Electric motors can recuperate some energy while slowing. Driving style, speed, weather and heating or cooling use all influence real-world range.

Do not compare WLTP and EPA range figures as if they were measured on the same cycle. Consult the current Audi technical data for the exact country, variant and model year.

06 / Audi S and RS

Two letters.
Different intent.

Both are performance-oriented Audi lines; the exact hardware depends on the model.

S
THE SPORTING STEP

Audi S

S models add performance and a more assertive setup to a standard Audi line. Engine, suspension, brakes and quattro equipment differ across the S range.

RS
AUDI SPORT

Audi RS

RS models are developed by Audi Sport GmbH for a more focused performance character. Model-specific powertrains, chassis tuning and design are not identical between vehicles.

10 / Motorsport

Built to compete.
Remembered for what it changed.

Audi’s competition history includes Auto Union’s Silver Arrows, quattro rallying, endurance racing at Le Mans, touring cars, Formula E and the Dakar Rally. Audi credits quattro rallying with helping transform the sport’s use of four-wheel drive.
